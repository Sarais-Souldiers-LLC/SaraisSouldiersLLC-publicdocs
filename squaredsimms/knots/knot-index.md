## 🔗 [Knot Families](ca://s?q=Explain_knot_families)
The SquaredSimms™ Universe organizes knots into distinct metaphysical families.  
Each family governs a different mode of binding, bridging, or stabilizing energy.

- **Ancestral Knots** — Bind lineage memory, heritage, and identity threads.  
- **Navigation Knots** — Anchor directional logic, pathfinding, and chamber traversal.  
- **Lexicon Knots** — Bind vocabulary, meaning, and semantic resonance.  
- **Cosmology Knots** — Tie together creation ladders, tetraktys engines, and universe scaffolding.  
- **Compliance Knots** — Bind governance, policy, and protection protocols.
